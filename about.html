Behind the Grid: The Journey to EdlynP

For over 20 years, my canvas wasn't a digital screen—it was occupational safety, environmental protection, and high-stakes readiness response for the United States Air Force. Retiring as a Master Sergeant (MSgt), my career was defined by strict systems, leading teams of up to 25 military and civilian personnel, and managing complex protocols for chemical, biological, radiological, and nuclear hazards. In that world, an unaligned detail wasn't just a design flaw; it was a critical failure point.

When I transitioned from military service to academia to pursue web development and digital design, the shift felt entirely natural. I discovered that writing clean HTML, structuring responsive CSS layout patterns, and wrestling with script variables required the exact same systematic mindset. Stepping into the classroom wasn't just about learning software like Photoshop or Illustrator; it was about translating two decades of strategic execution into visual and interactive experiences.

Whether leading a multi-disciplinary team or managing complex code lines, my mission remains unchanged: bringing order, clarity, and intentional structure to complex environments.
